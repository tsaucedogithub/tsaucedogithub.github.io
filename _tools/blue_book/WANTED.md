# Blue Book: passages wanted for the 24 books not on Project Gutenberg

These are the books from the top of the list whose text I cannot pull and verify myself. For each, I have named the three passages I want (two main passages for play, one famous passage used only as a hint). Please paste the exact text from your copy into one plain-text file per book at `_tools/blue_book/inbox/<id>.txt`, in this shape (the `===` lines are markers; keep the locus on them):

```
=== main1 | locus: Part 1, Chapter 7 ===
exact text of the passage, paragraphs separated by a blank line

=== main2 | locus: Part 3, Chapter 3 ===
exact text

=== famous | locus: Part 1, Chapter 1 ===
exact text
```

Keep the author's punctuation. Do not worry about em dashes (I convert every em dash, en dash and `--` to ` - ` when I load them). If a passage I named is longer than about 200 words, cut it at a sentence boundary; if you think a different passage is better, use yours and say so on the marker line (`=== main1 | locus: ... | note: swapped for the X scene ===`). 80 to 200 words each is the target; the famous one can be 40 to 160.

I will add the metadata (year, clue, aliases), write the significance blurbs, run the checks, and put them in rotation. Where I already know the exact first words, I give them; otherwise I describe the scene.
