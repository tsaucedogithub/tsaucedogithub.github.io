When I was 16 I got really into photography. I was convinced I'd become a National Geographic photographer, and on trips with my family would take photos like this and write pretentious captions about the significance of both the location and my documentation of it. I'm so glad I grew out of that habit.

Anyways, this is Polecat River, near Yellowstone. The yellow photo is Banff and the black and white one is Lake Louise.
